## Getting Started

Your first hour in Sample Game sets the tone for the entire run. Focus on these three things first.

### 1. Learn the Controls

The default keybinds cover movement, interaction and inventory. Spend two minutes in the settings menu before your first match.

### 2. Gather Early Resources

Wood and stone spawn near your starting area. Collect at least 20 of each before nightfall.

### 3. Avoid These Common Mistakes

- Spending currency before unlocking the shop discount
- Ignoring daily quests (they compound fast)
- Fighting the first boss under level 5
