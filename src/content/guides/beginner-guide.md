Fisch looks simple — cast, wait, reel — but underneath sits one of Roblox's deepest
value economies: 400,000+ fish variations from mutations and attributes stacking on
base species. Here's how to go from first cast to trading-ready without wasting your
early C$.

## The core loop

1. **Hold to cast**, wait for a bite — you can "shake" your rod to attract fish faster.
2. On a bite, **follow the blue line** with the white bar. Missing a few beats is fine;
   filling the bottom progress bar lands the catch.
3. Sell at the merchant, upgrade your rod, push to a new area. Repeat.

Every fish has a **base sell price modified by weight, mutation and attributes**. Two
identical species can differ in value by 10× or more — that's the entire game economy
in one sentence.

## Early money: rod upgrades beat everything

Your first upgrades matter more than anything you'll do later. The stat that pays is
**luck** — it drives rare species and mutation odds, and mutations are where value
multiplies (up to 12× for Aether — see the [full multiplier table](/mutations/)).

Don't overthink early choices: any purchasable rod in your stage bracket with a solid
luck stat carries you. The [rod tier list](/rod-tier-list/) shows all 248 rods with
lure/luck/control plus how to get each one — filter by your current stage and pick the
best luck you can afford.

## Mutations, attributes and appraisal

- A fish carries **one mutation** (multiplier from 0× to 12×).
- **Attributes stack** on top: Shiny, Sparkling, Big, Giant and friends can combine on
  a single fish.
- **Appraisal** rerolls the mutation for a fee — but attributes like Shiny and
  Sparkling are never lost during appraisal. The endgame move is stacking safe
  attributes, then rerolling toward a top multiplier.

Skip appraisal on cheap fish; the fee only makes sense on high base-value species where
the multiplier swing is worth real money.

## Codes: don't miss the flash drops

Fisch runs a **flash-code pattern**: most codes drop with weekly updates and die within
24-48 hours. A handful of long-term codes stay live for months. Check the
[codes page](/codes/) right after each weekly update lands — that timing is the whole
trick.

## When you're ready to trade

Trading is where mid-game money lives, but Fisch has three competing value systems and
they disagree by 30%+ on the same item. Before your first serious trade, read the
[trading guide](/guides/trading-guide/) and keep the [value list](/values/) open — it
cross-checks all three systems weekly and flags the items where they disagree.
